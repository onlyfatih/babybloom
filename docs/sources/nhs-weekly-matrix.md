# NHS weekly comparison matrix (weeks 4–40)

Source: NHS "Best Start in Life" week-by-week guide,
https://www.nhs.uk/best-start-in-life/pregnancy/week-by-week-guide-to-pregnancy/
Path per row is appended to that base. All 37 pages read on **2026-11-27**
(HTTP 200). Page metadata `article:modified_time` is 5 Feb 2026 for every
page except week 13 (10 Feb 2026). No page shows a "reviewed" date.

Objects and numbers are copied as written. Nothing was filled from memory.
Developmental and parent text on those pages is **not** copied into the app;
Turkish copy will be written separately and reviewed (content-model.md).

## Basis and convention codes

- **basis**: `size` = page says "the size of"; `length` = page says "the length
  of / same length as"; `size+weight` = page says "size and weight of".
  None of these is an individual measurement.
- **convention**: `CRL` = page says "from head to bottom" (crown-rump);
  `CHL` = "from head to heel / head to toe" (crown-heel); `unstated` = the
  page gives a length with no convention. Week 20's page says
  "Measurements are now taken from head to heel." The jump from 15.3 cm
  (week 19, CRL) to 25.6 cm (week 20, CHL) is this change, not growth.
- **weight**: no NHS week page gives a baby weight. No weights are shown.

## Gaps and handling

- Weeks 1–3: no NHS week page in this guide range; app shows "content starts at week 4".
- Weeks 41–42: outside the target range; app shows no comparison, no fabricated text.
- Lengths with `unstated` convention (4, 5, 6, 8, 13) are shown without a convention label and with "yaklaşık".
- Some later objects (watermelon at 39, pumpkin at 40) are much larger than
  the matching lengths; NHS uses them as general size comparisons. The app
  says "yaklaşık ... büyüklüğünde" and never "gerçek boyut".
